# Secrets (from online guides only; nothing here was verified on the emulator)

What the guides say, condensed. Sources: WiKirby "Vegetable Valley - Stage 1", "Vegetable Valley - Stage 2",
"HAL Room" (read 2026-11-10), and guide snippets from a web search (ludo.guide, gamesurge). No inputs, frames or
coordinates come from any existing movie.

## 1-1: the invisible door to the UFO room
- Where: the **second area** of Vegetable Valley stage 1 (the waterfalls over pits). "An invisible doorway can be
  found beneath the leftmost block in the waterfall."
- Visual clue on the NES: that block has **no shading** beneath it (the others do). Another guide describes "a
  dark shade of water" under the first block of the waterfall.
- How to enter, per one guide: "inflate and float under the left-most block to be whisked away to a secret room";
  another says "find a secret door while floating. Enter". The guides disagree on whether Up is needed.
- What is there: "a small room with a UFO inside" (a single UFO enemy). Inhaling and swallowing it gives the UFO
  ability, which normally cannot be found this early. Nothing else is in the room.
- UFO Kirby flies freely (D-pad), shoots with B (longer holds charge stronger shots), cannot drop through thin
  floors or climb down ladders, and loses the ability when the stage ends.

## 1-2: the HAL room (behind the Warp Star)
- Where: Vegetable Valley stage 2, the **first area** (the grassy walkway with the invincible Shotzo and Gordo),
  beyond the Warp Star at the end of the path. Normally touching the Warp Star carries Kirby to the next area.
- The trick, per WiKirby: when the Warp Star is reached (in view), Kirby "has to go back to the left side of the
  screen", "to the precise point when the Warp Star disappears from view", then return toward it. "If done
  correctly, the star will be gone, and Kirby will be able to proceed further right to reach the formation of
  Star Blocks that spell out 'HAL', with a Broom Hatter sweeping on the L-letter blocks."
- "Once Kirby returns to the left, the Warp Star will be there again."
- It has no items or rewards: an Easter egg.

## 1-2: the hidden doorway in the final cave
- "A hidden doorway in the final cave leads to a waterfall room with a Bronto Burt and a 1-Up."
